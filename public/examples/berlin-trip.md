## How to read the day tables

- Each row is a **destination**. Start / End / Duration = time spent there. **Transport / Travel time / Travel details** = how you get *to* this row's place from the previous row. First row of a day has no transport.
- Travel times are Google Maps public transport (daytime). *(estimate)* = not checked in Maps. No car.
- A 7-day ticket ABC (BER is zone C) is usually cheaper than single tickets *(check BVG)*.
- Option A / B rows (Thursday evening) are alternatives — pick one.

## Bags & luggage plan

- **Bring a small daypack** for water, power bank, rain layer, ID, cash + card (many Imbiss stands are cash only).
- **Tue:** go to the hotel first with the suitcase, then explore.
- **Wed:** long day in Mitte, no hotel stop until evening.
- **Thu:** the hotel is 5 min from Museum Island — drop shopping at lunchtime if needed.
- **Fri:** east side + Kreuzberg, back to the hotel before dinner.
- **Sat:** KaDeWe shopping → carry it back before dinner.
- **Sun:** check out with the suitcase straight to BER.
